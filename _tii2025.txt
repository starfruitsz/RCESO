Adaptive Parameter Estimation Based Nonlinear
Active Disturbance Rejection Controller for a
PMSM System
Honghao Xia , Shengquan Li , Senior Member, IEEE , Zhengrong Xiang , Member, IEEE ,
Donglei Chen, and Zhe Li
Abstract— Considering total disturbances, i.e., parameter
variation, model error, and load mutation, an adaptive ac-
tive disturbance rejection control (adaptive ADRC) method
is proposed to regulate the speed of permanent magnet
synchronous motor system (PMSMs) in this article. First,
a tracking state space model of a PMSMs is structured with
adaptive parameter estimation of inertia and ﬂux. Second,
a nonlinear extended state observer (ESO) based on an
adaptive law is designed to estimate the total disturbances
for feedforward compensation. A ﬁnite time bounded stabil-
ity theory is established to analyze the proposed nonlinear
ESO. In addition, the stability of the closed-loop system is
proved by a Lyapunov stability theory based on a proposed
positive deﬁnite scalar function. Finally, the speed regu-
lation and antidisturbance performances of the proposed
adaptive ADRC controller are veriﬁed by an experimental
platform based on DSPF28335 and MATLAB/SIMULINK. The
reliability and superiority of the proposed controller are
veriﬁed by simulation and experiment results.
Index Terms— Adaptive active disturbance rejection con-
trol (Adaptive ADRC), adaptive law, parameter variations,
permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM), system
veriﬁcations.

ERMANENT magnet synchronous motor system
(PMSMs) has been widely used in military and industrial
ﬁelds due to its advantages of high air gap ﬂux density, high
torque capacity, and high power density [1], [2]. Unfortunately,
in practical industrial productions, the speed regulation
performance of PMSMs will be degraded due to the unknown
parameters and total disturbances [3]. Conventional control
algorithm is difﬁcult to obtain excellent anti-disturbance ability

Thus, parameter identiﬁcation and adaptive control technolo-
gies are effective ways to solve the problem of unknown pa-
rameters for PMSMs in recent years. In [5], a novel ﬁnite-time
extended state observer based on an inertia identiﬁcation method
is developed to real-time calculate the true inertia value. In [6],
a variable structure extended sliding mode observer for load
torque observation is designed to simultaneously realize identi-
ﬁcation of load torque and rotational inertia. Parameter identi-
ﬁcation method often depends on precise mathematical model,
while accurate system model is difﬁcult to obtain in practical
systems [7]. Adaptive control technologies have been gradually
applied to solve unknown parameters for PMSMs, which can
ﬂexibly adjust its own parameters according to the variations
of the environment [8], [9], [10].I n [11], an adaptive nonlinear
control with a three-layer neural network approximator is pro-
posed to improve speed tracking performance by using nonlinear
information gain subjected load torque variation. Meanwhile,
the application of observer in adaptive control technologies can
enhance the ability to estimate the unknown disturbances and
improve the control accuracy of PMSMs [12], [13].I n [14],
an adaptive sliding mode control law based on a disturbance
observer is proposed to improve the ability of suppressing the
load disturbance for PMSMs. However, model-based-observer
control technologies are often limited due to their sensitivity to
perturbations of model parameters in engineering applications.
Active disturbance rejection controller (ADRC), i.e.,
extended-state-observer (ESO) based controller, independent on
system model, originated with a nonlinear control structure, is an
alternative disturbance estimation technology with advantages
of high precision and fast response in speed tracking [15], [16].
However, the conventional ADRC is often designed with the
ﬁxed observer bandwidth and gains [17], [18]. It is difﬁcult to
achieve satisfactory speed regulation performance in the face
of unknown parameters, which will also limit some practical
industrial applications [19], [20].I n [21], an adaptive ADRC
controller based on generalized integrator ESO is proposed to
solve the instability problem caused by torque ripple. In [22],a n
ESO is optimized by an adaptive resonant controller to attenuate
both the aperiodic disturbances and periodic uncertainties. These
adaptive ADRC controllers can suppress the uncertain ripple

In this article, an adaptive ADRC controller is proposed to
enhance the speed regulation and anti-disturbance ability of
PMSMs under the double-loop structure. The main contributions
of this article are summarized as follows.
1) The conventional approach to solve unknown parameters
is based on parameter identiﬁcation. However, the param-
eter identiﬁcation often depends on precise mathematical
model. In this article, a tracking state space model of a
PMSMs is structured with adaptive parameter estimation
of inertia and ﬂux.
2) Compared to [24], this article combines adaptive laws
that derived from the stability of the entire closed-loop
system by cooperating ADRC for total disturbances. Fur-
thermore, the parameter selection of the adaptive law also
takes into account the system performance.

modeling of PMSM is presented in Section II. The design

design to verify the reliability and superiority of the proposed
speed controller. Finally, Section V concludes this article.

D. Parameter Selection Guidelines
First, the coefﬁcients k1, k2, and k3 of the designed adaptive
law need to satisfy (30). Then, system stability, estimation’s pre-
cision and antidisturbance ability should be taken into account.
The parameters tuning guidelines are summarized as following
section.
1) The selection of parameters ω0 necessitates a careful con-
sideration of both the stability of system and the precision
of the observer [29].
2) The coefﬁcient kp is adjusted to strike a balance between
response dynamics and overshoot.
3) The parameter r of TD should be carefully selected to bal-
ance computational efﬁciency and tracking performance.
Finally, the parameters will be validated and reﬁned through
simulation and experimental tests.

C. Experimental Platform Setup
According to Fig. 16, the experimental platform is mainly
composed of PMSM, DSP control chip, peripheral circuit, in-
verter and host computer. The DSP control chip transmits in-
structions and uploads data with the host computer through SCI
module. Based on analog-to-digital converter (ADC) module
and peripheral circuit, the detections of dc-bus volt age and line
current during motor operation are realized. The PWM module is
controlled to generate a complete vector-controlled space vector
pulse width modulation (SVPWM) wave, which is output to the
inverter to produce the circular rotating magnetic ﬁeld required
for PMSM operation. Through quadrature encoded pulses (QEP)
module, the feedback signal of photoelectric encoder on PMSM
is collected and counted to obtain the position and speed infor-
mation of PMSM in operation. The function of the hysteresis
dynamometer is to applied as an inherent load for PMSM.

V. C ONCLUSION
The problem of unknown parameters is solved by constructing
an adaptive law to estimate the variations of parameters in this
article. Meanwhile, a nonlinear ESO is designed to estimate the
total disturbances for feedforward compensation. In addition,
the stability of the closed-loop system is proved by a Lyapunov
stability theory based on the proposed positive deﬁnite scalar
function. According to the experiment results, the speed re-
sponse characteristics and anti-disturbance ability of the adap-
tive ADRC have been enhanced by 53% and 22%, respectively.
Compared to the conventional ADRC controller, the proposed
adaptive ADRC controller not only offers improved speed reg-
ulation performance but also exhibits stronger anti-disturbance
ability. Both simulation and experimental results demonstrate
the effectiveness and superiority of the proposed adaptive ADRC
controller. In addition, the shortcoming of the adaptive ADRC
controller proposed in this article is mainly manifested in the
following aspect. Due to the unknown parameters of PMSMs,
some motor parameters are estimated by adaptive law in this
article, but these still need to be optimized, such as incorporating
certain punitive mechanisms. Future work will focus on the anal-
ysis of adaptive parameters and the design of bandwidth of ESO
to develop control performance for engineering applications. In
addition, it is necessary to compare with other control schemes
to enhance performance, practicality, and range of application.
